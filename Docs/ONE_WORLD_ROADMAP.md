# ONE WORLD — Product Roadmap

## North star

A persistent-feeling, systemic social world in which players create lives and stories. Launch scope is Lagos; long-term scope is Nigeria (36 states plus FCT), then a connected global world.

## Stage 0 — Project stewardship

- [x] Establish a dedicated development branch.
- [x] Preserve the inherited Unreal Engine project and attribution.
- [x] Establish ONE WORLD identity and project documentation.
- [ ] Open and build the project in the target Unreal Engine 5.8 installation.
- [ ] Record baseline packaged-build performance and known issues.

## Stage 1 — Playable Lagos foundation

- [ ] Confirm a clean editor build and playable startup map.
- [ ] Create a small representative Lagos district with clear boundaries.
- [ ] Player profile, six needs, money and save/load.
- [ ] Interactable locations and simple activity loop.
- [ ] Basic pedestrian schedules and local ambient activity.
- [ ] First Danfo/Keke passenger interaction.
- [ ] Keyboard and controller input validation.

## Stage 2 — Living city systems

- [ ] NPC daily routines, occupations, homes and schedules.
- [ ] Public transport routes, stops, fares, boarding and alighting.
- [ ] Traffic rules, congestion, incidents, breakdowns and recovery.
- [ ] Vendors, markets, shops, services and dynamic prices.
- [ ] Weather and time-of-day effects.
- [ ] Emergency services and contextual conflict resolution.

## Stage 3 — Social and economic life

- [ ] Jobs, education, applications, shifts and salary.
- [ ] Player businesses, employees, inventory and cash flow.
- [ ] Housing, rent, property purchase and construction.
- [ ] Friends, groups, relationships, messaging and shared activities.
- [ ] Multiplayer architecture and abuse/safety controls.

## Stage 4 — Lagos expansion

- [ ] District content packs with distinct identities.
- [ ] More landmarks, transport corridors and local activities.
- [ ] Streaming, NPC population tiers and performance budgets.
- [ ] Player-generated stories and opt-in sharing.

## Stage 5 — Nigeria

- [ ] Regional content schema and validation.
- [ ] Abuja and additional state pilots.
- [ ] All 36 states and FCT, introduced progressively.
- [ ] Inter-state travel, regional economies and cultural variation.

## Stage 6 — Global expansion

- [ ] Country and city content packs.
- [ ] Localization, regional rules and economic variation.
- [ ] Cross-region travel and scalable online services.

## Release discipline

Each milestone requires a reproducible build, a test checklist, performance notes, asset licence review and a clear list of known limitations. A roadmap item is not complete merely because a placeholder exists.
